# Changelog

## v2.0.0 (31 October 2026): release for the CATENA submission

Archived on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23813801. Repository: https://github.com/Quist-ish/volta-drought-propagation.

Accompanies *Regime shift, rising runoff efficiency and sparse gauging: limits to testing drought propagation in a Sahel–savanna landscape*.

Added
* Trend, change-point and rainfall–runoff analyses (objective i): `climate_trends_teleconnections.py`,
  `runoff_coefficient.py`.
* Wavelet coherence, seasonal correlations and event-rate models with six climate indices (objective ii):
  `climate_trends_teleconnections.py`, `seasonal_correlations.py`; ATL3 is computed from ERSSTv5.
* Common-reference, equivalence (TOST), synthetic-truth and channel-distance analyses (objective iii):
  `reference_equivalence_simulation.py`; stability, severity and antecedent-state analyses of the per-link estimate:
  `perlink_sensitivity.py`; year-displacement synchrony test and estimator precision:
  `year_shift_precision.py`; per-link comparison with the released Ebro link table:
  `ebro_link_parity.py`.
* CMIP6 retrieval pinned to logged store paths and versions, quantile delta mapping, the
  leakage-free hybrid wavelet–machine learning transfer model, projections and the monitoring
  horizon (objective iv): `fetch_cmip6.py`, `projection_cmip6.py`, `monitoring_horizon.py`.
* Publication figures under the article numbers (`figures/`, `figures_pub/collect_final_figures.py`).
  Distribution and uncertainty marks (raincloud plots, one dot per model, gradient intervals) are in
  `figures_pub/pubviz.py`; no figure uses dashed or dotted lines, and the export refuses one that does.
* `MANIFEST.md` and `manifest/*.csv`: provenance of every table and figure.
* `verify_manuscript_numbers.py`: reported numbers checked against the derived tables.
* Sensitivity extensions (`sensitivity_extensions.py`): the Ebro thinning summary with draw counts and
  percentiles (Table S23); wild cluster bootstrap, latitude, rainfall and gauge-specific tests of runoff at
  fixed rainfall, with drainage-area and storage-signature checks (Table S7, Section S3); trend-adjusted,
  detrended and out-of-sample AMO event-rate tests (Table S10); matching at drought thresholds −1.0 and
  −1.5, without gap filling and under a regime-conditional index (Table S16); a downstream-gauge cluster
  bootstrap of the per-link estimate; and block-bootstrap change points. `projection_post1970.py`
  retrains the transfer model on 1970–2007 only.
* Fig. 2 (`figures_pub/fig02_framework_redraw.py`), Figs. S9, S17 and S18 (`figures_pub/figS_redesign.py`)
  and the graphical abstract (`figures_pub/graphical_abstract.py`).
* Continuous integration (`.github/workflows/verify.yml`): checksums, unit tests and the number check on
  every push and pull request.
* `DATA.md`, `LICENSE-DATA` (CC BY 4.0 for derived data), `.zenodo.json`.

Changed
* `reproduce.sh` runs the stages in dependency order (reanalysis networks before the analyses that
  read them; GPCC subset before the rainfall analyses) and ends with the number check.
* `download_inputs.py` also retrieves ERSSTv5 and, when missing, the NOAA PSL index files.
* Pinned environment extended with `pymannkendall`, `PyWavelets`, `pycwt`, `xgboost`, `gcsfs`,
  `cftime`, `numcodecs`, `PyYAML`.
* Figure scripts that plot discharge (Figs. S5 and S19) stop with a message when the GRDC records
  are absent; Fig. 1 uses shipped station means in that case.

Removed from the release
* Analyses that the article no longer reports after its consolidation (27 October 2026): the
  raw-discharge Ebro concurrence check (CEDEX input), SIEREM, mapped-distance, intervening-catchment
  and month-level models, and the distance figure script (`figS21_distance.py`); only the derived
  tables that article figures read are shipped in `figures_pub/supp_tables/`.
* Rendering and packaging scripts of the earlier version of the study
  (`render_observational_figures.py`, `render_supp_figures.py`, `render_r6_figures.py`,
  `build_supplement.py`, `export_submission_figures.py`), whose outputs used an older numbering.
* The GPCC Volta subset (regenerated by `subset_gpcc.py` from the open download).

## v1.0.0 (14 October 2026)

First release, for an earlier version of the study (event-based tracking of drought propagation on the
Volta network, identifiability, reanalysis benchmark). Not cited by the CATENA article.
